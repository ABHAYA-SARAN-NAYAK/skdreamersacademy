About SK Dreamers Academy

SK Dreamers Academy began with a simple belief: every child learns differently, and every child deserves a teacher who notices that. What started as a small skill-development initiative in Thiruvottiyur has grown into a trusted academy offering ten specialised courses for children and a recognised Montessori Teacher Training program for aspiring educators.

We are proud to be an Authorised Public Skill Institute of the World Skill Council (WSC), London, UK - a standard we hold ourselves to in every classroom, every day. Our approach blends structured curriculum with individual attention, so no child is just another face in a crowded batch.

Our Mission

To provide every child with a strong academic and creative foundation through personalised, activity-based learning - and to train the next generation of confident, well-equipped educators.

Our Vision

To be Chennai's most trusted name in child skill development and Montessori teacher training, known for the individual care we give every student and every trainee.
