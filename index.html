Who We Are

A Place Built for Curious Minds.

SK Dreamers Academy is an inspiring education centre and an Authorised Public Skill Institute of the World Skill Council (WSC), London, UK, offering comprehensive Child Skill Development Courses and International-Standard Montessori Teacher Training programs.

With a strong foundation in Montessori education, professional teacher training and skill development, we provide structured learning opportunities for both children and aspiring educators.
