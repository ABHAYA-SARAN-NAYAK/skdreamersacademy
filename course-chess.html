Chess

A structured chess program that builds patience, strategic thinking, and decision-making skills through progressive, age-appropriate coaching. Students learn openings, tactics, and endgame fundamentals while developing focus and sportsmanship.

Ages 6-16 Years 2 Classes / Week Beginner to Intermediate

What Your Child Will Learn

✓
Openings and Fundamentals: Board setup, piece movement, and foundational opening principles
✓
Tactics and Combinations: Forks, pins, skewers, and pattern recognition for middlegame play
✓
Endgame Strategy: King and pawn endings, checkmate patterns, and positional evaluation
✓
Focus and Sportsmanship: Clock management, competitive etiquette, and tournament preparation
✓
Progressive Levels: Beginner to intermediate, with opportunities to compete in inter-academy matches as skills progress
✓
Schedule: 2 classes per week, age-appropriate batches for focused coaching
