# SK Dreamers Academy Website

Affiliated with **World Skill Council (WSC), London UK**, SK Dreamers Academy in Tiruvottiyur, Chennai offers premier skill-building courses for children (ages 3–16) and AMI-standard Montessori Teacher Training for aspiring educators.

---

## 🚀 Features & Highlights

- **Soft Neo-Brutalist Design**: Vibrant color palette (Deep Royal Purple `#6B21A8`, Warm Gold `#F59E0B`), flat offset ink shadows, thick borders, scrapbooks, and interactive micro-animations.
- **10 Core Children's Skill Programs**:
  1. Phonics & Early Reading
  2. Spoken English & Public Speaking
  3. Abacus Mental Math
  4. Drawing & Painting
  5. Hindi Language
  6. Creative Art & Craft
  7. Handwriting Master
  8. Chess Strategy
  9. Carrom Coaching
  10. Academic Tuition (Grades 1–10)
- **Certified Montessori Teacher Training**: AMI & APTTI aligned diploma programs for mothers, teachers, and school owners.
- **Full Multi-Page Structure**:
  - `index.html` (Homepage with Scrapbook Slider, Counter, Testimonials & FAQs)
  - `about.html` (Founding Story, Leadership Profile & Credentials)
  - `courses.html` (Full Course Catalog)
  - `montessori-training.html` (Teacher Training Admissions & Curriculum)
  - `gallery.html` (Filterable Photo Gallery)
  - `contact.html` (Live Google Maps Embed & Interactive Inquiry Form)
  - `faq.html` (Accordion FAQs)
  - 10 Dedicated Course Detail Pages
- **Fully Responsive**: Verified down to 320px screen width with mobile navigation drawer.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **HTML5**: Semantic markup
- **CSS3**: Pure Vanilla CSS design tokens, CSS Grid & Flexbox
- **JavaScript**: Pure Vanilla JS (Navigation drawer, Course slider scroll sync, Counter animation, Gallery filters)

## 📂 Project Structure

```text
skdreameracademy/
├── css/
│   ├── styles.css        # Global CSS Tokens, Base & Reset, Neo-Brutalist UI
│   ├── navbar.css        # Navigation Bar & Mobile Drawer Panel
│   ├── hero.css          # Hero Section, Stats Bar & Media Frames
│   ├── courses.css       # Course Scrapbook Slider & Listing Cards
│   ├── course-detail.css # Detail Page Layouts & Enrolment Forms
│   └── animations.css   # Scroll Reveal & Hover Micro-animations
├── js/
│   ├── main.js           # Scroll Reveal Observer & Form Handlers
│   ├── navbar.js         # Navigation Scroll Shadow & Mobile Drawer
│   ├── courses-slider.js # Course Scrapbook Slider & Progress Bar Sync
│   ├── counter.js        # Animated Stats Counter
│   └── gallery.js        # Category Filterable Gallery Grid
├── images/               # Asset fallback backups
├── *.html                # Site pages & course details
└── README.md
```
