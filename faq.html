Help & Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our courses, admissions, and certifications.

What age groups are eligible for your courses?
▼
Our skill programs cater to children aged 3 to 16 years. Phonics starts from 3 years, Abacus & Spoken English from 5 years, Chess & Carrom from 6 years, and Academic Tuition covers Grades 1 to 10. Montessori Teacher Training is for adults (18+ yrs).
What is the maximum batch size at SK Dreamers Academy?
▼
We maintain small, focused batch sizes of 10 to 12 students per class. This guarantees individual attention for every child and allows our certified teachers to pace lessons according to each child's learning speed.
Are your certificates internationally recognized?
▼
Yes! SK Dreamers Academy is affiliated with the World Skill Council (WSC), London UK. Upon completing any course or our Montessori Teacher Training program, students receive official certification accredited by WSC London.
What are the qualifications of the Montessori trainer?
▼
Our Montessori Teacher Training program is personally led by founder B. Saranya, who is AMI Trained (South Korea) and APTTI Certified (Canada) with over 10 years of experience in early childhood education.
Are weekend batches available for working parents and adults?
▼
Yes, we offer both weekday and weekend batch options across all courses and for the Montessori Teacher Training diploma to accommodate busy schedules.
How do I book a demo class for my child?
▼
You can book a demo class by submitting the quick enquiry form on our website or calling us directly at 8148904335 / 9514571990. Our team will schedule a session at your convenience.
